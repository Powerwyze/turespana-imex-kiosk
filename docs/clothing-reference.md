# Client clothing reference

The six-panel client sheet for Canarias, Barcelona, Bilbao, Madrid, Andalucía and Valencia is retained privately as assets/costume-reference.jpg for design provenance. Destination costumePrompt fields transcribe its garment shapes, colours, embroidery and layering. Headwear is optional and must not obscure the captured guest.

Only the guest camera photo and established painted-style reference are sent to the portrait service. The wardrobe sheet is not sent: its twelve models introduced a risk of extra people, caught by the foreground-guest safeguard during a live check. Detailed garment instructions preserve the intended outfits without introducing those reference identities. Guest face, hair and expression preservation remain the priority.

The second client image guides the Spain-themed kiosk background. It does not replace the established portrait-rendering style.
