# Regional photographic portraits

Guest facial identity, body proportions, source hair color, and natural hairline remain protected. The requested 10–15% beauty retouch is unchanged. Hair arrangement and region-specific accessories are intentionally transformed along with clothing.

Each destination has one shared hairPrompt in public/data/destinations.json. Both the image prompt and independent visual checker receive that exact module. Missing defining hair/accessories fails quality validation; rejection never automatically purchases another image.

Valencia dress: traditional three-bun Fallera styling, side rodetes, rear braided bun and peinetas. Natural camera perspective may hide the rear bun. Madrid dress: white pañuelo, gathered bun and the client's expressly requested red rose. Official Madrid descriptions traditionally specify a carnation; the rose is the client's creative direction. Trouser ensembles have their own region-specific headwear and are not assigned dress hairstyles.
